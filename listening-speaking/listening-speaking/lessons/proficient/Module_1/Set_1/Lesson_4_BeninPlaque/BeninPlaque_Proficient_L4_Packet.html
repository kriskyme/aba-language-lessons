Good to Know

The Kingdom of Benin was a West African state whose capital, Benin City, is in what is now southern Nigeria. Its ruler is called the oba. It is not the modern Republic of Benin, which is a separate country to the west of Nigeria and takes its name from the same bay.

Wole Soyinka is a Nigerian poet and playwright. In 1986 he became the first African writer to be awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature.

The speaker and the museum. This series was made by BBC Radio 4 together with the British Museum, and every object in the series comes from that museum's collection. Neil MacGregor, who wrote and presented it, was the director of the British Museum at the time. The British Museum holds Benin plaques that were taken from Benin City in 1897.

Words to Know

1. plaque a flat piece of metal, stone, or wood, fixed to a wall, usually carrying a picture or lettering
2. relief a carving or casting in which the figures stand out from a flat background, so the surface is raised rather than flat
3. commodities goods that are bought, sold, and shipped in quantity, such as metal, cloth, or shells
4. prohibition an official rule forbidding something completely
5. punitive intended as a punishment
6. artefacts objects made by people, especially ones kept and studied for what they show about a culture. Also spelled artifacts
Phrase Spotlight
cut out the middle-men: to remove the people who used to stand between a buyer and a seller, so the two now deal directly.
not least: a way of adding a final item to a list while marking it as the most important one, not the least.

Learn the Phrase
Handing On Someone Else's Words

The speaker never lets a quotation stand on its own. Something before it says whose it is, and something after it says what it proves. Here are three of his own moves.

Say who is about to speak, and why they are worth hearing. "a wound that's still felt keenly today, as Wole Soyinka, the Nigerian poet and playwright, describes."

Say what kind of thing they said, before they say it. "The overall effect was enthusiastically described in detail by an early Dutch visitor."

Boil a long quotation down to one sentence afterwards. "In short, Europeans visiting Benin, in the fifteenth and sixteenth century, discovered a society every bit as organised and structured as the great courts of Europe."

Frames to use: As ___, the ___, describes: ___. / ___ was described in detail by ___. / In short, ___.
