Good to Know

Unit 1B: Telling What Someone Said

Listening & Speaking · Narrating Intermediate 2.1.1.1

Objective: I can put what another person said into one short sentence of my own.

Learn the Phrase
Saying What Someone Said

In the story, Darrell keeps the exact words because they were short: "And the voice said, leave him alone," and "Marcus looked at me and said, come on."

The person who introduces the story puts years into one sentence instead: "The family moved every few years, and being the new kid often meant he was lonely."

Use these when it is your turn: He said, "___." · What she said was that ___. · In one sentence: ___. · Basically, he told me to ___.

Say It Like a Native Speaker
Two Words, Pushed Hard

When a speaker puts two things against each other, the voice pushes harder on the word that carries the difference. Darrell does it here: "It wasn't loud. It was calm but firm."

1.
Listen to the line twice and underline the word your teacher pushes hardest in each sentence. Then say both sentences to your partner the same way.
"It wasn't loud. It was calm but firm."
