Good to Know

REI has been an outdoor gear store since 1938. It's a co-op, which means shoppers who join as members can earn a yearly reward based on what they buy.

A liter is a metric unit of volume. A backpack's capacity in liters tells you how much space is inside it, not how heavy it is.

Unit 3B: How to Choose a Backpack

Objective: produce a short description that puts steps in order, using words like "first," "once," and "then."

Learn the Phrase
Sequencing Language

When you explain how to do something, it helps to make the order clear. In the guide, REI does this: "There are three main areas where you'll need to make choices." Later, it says: "Once you've chosen the type of backpack you want, it's time to fit your pack."

Example: "First, decide how many nights you'll be gone. Once you know that, it's time to pick the size."

Now it's your turn. In the tasks below, you'll describe the steps for choosing a backpack, in order, using frames like these:

  • • "First, ___. Next, ___."
  • • "Once you've ___, it's time to ___."
  • • "After that, ___."

Say It Like a Native Speaker

Speakers often link words together smoothly instead of pausing between them. Practice this sentence: "Once you've chosen the type of backpack you want, it's time to fit your pack." Underline the places where you think the words link together.

Discuss It

  • ★★★★ "First, ___. After that, ___. The tricky part is ___, because ___."
