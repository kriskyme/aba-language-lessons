Good to Know

"Flightseeing" is not a standard English word. It is a word people in Alaska made from "flight" and "sightseeing," for a short plane or helicopter trip taken only to look at scenery that has no roads.

A hectare is a measure of land area, about the size of two football fields. This park covers more than 5.3 million of them.

Objective: I can disagree with someone by giving them something first and only then saying where I differ.

Learn the Phrase
Giving Ground, Then Differing

Listen again to the opening. The speaker gives away a weak point before anyone can use it against her, and then turns it: "Most Americans do not know its name. But Wrangell-St. Elias contains some of North America's largest glaciers and volcanoes." That is the same shape a polite disagreement takes in conversation.

Now it's your turn. In the tasks below, you'll practice this using lines like these:

  • • "I agree, and ___."
  • • "That's true, but ___."
  • • "I see what you mean, though ___."
  • • "I'm not so sure, because ___."
  • • "Exactly, and that's why ___."
