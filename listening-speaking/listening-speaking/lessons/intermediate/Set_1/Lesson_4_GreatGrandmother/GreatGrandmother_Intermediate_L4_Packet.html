Unit 4B: Great-Grandmother Learns English

Objective: ask for clarification when you don't understand something, using natural, polite phrases.

Learn the Phrase
Asking for Clarification

In this story, Natsuko sends her grandmother a new English word every day, and they work on phrases together. Learning something new like this means you'll sometimes need to ask someone to explain again.

Example: "What does 'challenge' mean?" ... "Could you say that again? I didn't catch it."

Now it's your turn. In the tasks below, you'll practice asking for clarification using frames like these:

  • • "What does ___ mean?"
  • • "Could you say that again?"
  • • "Sorry, could you repeat that?"
