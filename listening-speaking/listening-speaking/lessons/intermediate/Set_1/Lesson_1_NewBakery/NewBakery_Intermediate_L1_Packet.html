Unit 1B: New Bakery, Old Baking Method

Objective: describe and compare two things, naming a feature of each and giving a reason for the difference.

Learn the Phrase
Making Comparisons

In the audio, Sam Fromartz says: "The grains will be different in each farm." The audio also describes Seylou as "different from most bakeries around the country."

Use these frames to make your own comparisons:

  • • "It is ___, not ___."
  • • "___ is different from ___ because ___."
  • • "Unlike most ___, this one ___."

Say It Like a Native Speaker

Speakers stress the important words in a sentence, not every word. Practice this sentence: "Bethony uses about 20 different kinds of organic grains." Circle the words you think should be said louder: uses · 20 · different · organic · grains.

Discuss It

Get into a small group. Your group will get one question below. Take turns discussing it out loud, using a starter phrase from the list. While your group talks, keep a tally: how many times does someone use a comparison phrase like "different from" or "unlike"?

1.
Is Seylou's approach worth the extra effort, compared to a typical bakery?
2.
Would you rather eat at Seylou or a typical bakery? Why?
3.
What is one thing Seylou does that you think other bakeries should copy?

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "It is ___, not ___."
  • ★★ "___ is different from ___."
  • ★★★ "I think ___ is better because ___."
  • ★★★★ "Unlike most ___, ___ is ___, and I think that's ___ because ___."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, pick two things you know well (two foods, two stores, two hobbies). Describe one feature of each and compare them out loud.

  • ★★ "___ is like ___ because ___, but ___ is different because ___."
  • ★★★★ "___ and ___ are both ___, but ___ is more ___ than ___ because ___."
