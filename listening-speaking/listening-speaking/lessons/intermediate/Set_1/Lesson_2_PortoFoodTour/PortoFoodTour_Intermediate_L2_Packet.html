Unit 2A: A Food Tour Through Porto's Old Market

Listening & Speaking Intermediate S1.2.0

Objective: listen for examples that support a bigger idea, notice the details that confirm it, and compare a historic stop to something more typical by naming a feature of each and a reason for the difference.

Good to Know

Porto is a real historic city in northern Portugal, famous for its food and for port wine, which is actually named after the city.

A "tasca" is a small, traditional Portuguese tavern - a simple, casual place to eat and drink, found all over Portugal, not just in Porto.

Unit 2B: A Food Tour Through Porto's Old Market

Objective: give a specific example to support a bigger point, the way a guide points to one real stop to show off a whole city's food culture.

Learn the Phrase
Giving Examples

Andre's whole tour works this way: instead of just saying "Porto has great food," he takes visitors to specific, named stops - the sardine stand, the canned-seafood shop, the old grocery store - as real examples.

Example: "For example, the old grocery store has stayed open since 1896, which shows how much history is packed into this one market."

Now it's your turn. In the tasks below, you'll give your own examples using frames like these:

  • • "For example, ___."
  • • "Like ___, for instance."
  • • "Take ___, for example."
