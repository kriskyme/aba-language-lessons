Listening Test: Module 1 Set 1 Assessment

You'll listen to: "Visitors Laugh Away Troubles at the HaHaHouse Museum," an audio from VOA Learning English about a new museum in Zagreb, Croatia built entirely around laughter.

Words to Know

1. museuma building that holds many exhibits about a subject people are interested in
2. negativityan attitude that only sees the bad qualities in something
3. interactivedesigned to respond to what a person does
4. embraceto accept something readily and gladly
5. tickleto make someone laugh by lightly touching them
