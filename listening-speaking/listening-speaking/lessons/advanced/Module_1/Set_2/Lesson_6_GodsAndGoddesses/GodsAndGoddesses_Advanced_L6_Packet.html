Good to Know

The Museum of Fine Arts in Boston reopened five rebuilt galleries of ancient Greek, Roman, and Byzantine art in late 2021, after years of planning. This segment was filmed in those galleries.

Epidaurus was a healing sanctuary in ancient Greece where sick people travelled to ask for a cure. Asclepius was the god of medicine, and Hygeia was the goddess of health.

The museum's Athena Parthenos is a small Roman copy of a colossal statue of Athena that once stood in the Parthenon in Athens and has been lost for centuries.

Learn the Phrase
Sequencing Language

Jared Bowen runs a whole life story backwards in one sentence, and the order words are what keep it followable: "She descended from the sky into the museum 10 years ago, after standing anonymously in a suburban Boston estate for more than a century."

Laure Marest sets a "then" against a "now" with a single word: "Originally, it was very brightly painted." Bowen puts the museum's methods in order the same way: "To determine how the statue originally looked, the museum employed a host of methods from old-school scrutiny," then "To special lighting, photographic techniques and chemical analysis."

Now it's your turn. In the tasks below, you'll put things in order using frames like these:

  • • "Originally, ___."
  • • "___ years later, ___."
  • • "After ___, it ___."
  • • "From ___ to ___, ___."
  • • "It ended up ___."
