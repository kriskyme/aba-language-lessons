Speaking Task: Module 1 Set 1 Assessment

Topic: describe a workspace or creative space that matters to you (your own, or one you know well because someone described it to you), comparing it to a more ordinary version of that kind of space, and giving your honest opinion of it without stating that opinion outright.

Speaking Task ★

Target length: approx. 60-75 seconds.

  1. 1.
    Describe the space using at least 2 original features, in your own words.
  2. 2.
    Include 1 comparison between it and a more typical version of that kind of space, with 1 brief stated reason for the comparison. Use your own connector, not a memorized frame.

Before you present, check:

  • 2 or more specific features clearly described
  • Clear comparison with a specific stated reason
  • Mostly your own original connectors, not a memorized frame

Speaking Task ★★

Target length: approx. 90 seconds.

  1. 1.
    Organize your description around at least 3 details (what it's for, what's inside, who uses it).
  2. 2.
    Include at least 1 comparison to a more typical version of that kind of space.
  3. 3.
    Use at least 1 word that shows your opinion of the space without saying that opinion directly.

Before you present, check:

  • 3 or more details, clearly organized
  • Comparison integrated smoothly into the description
  • Evaluative word used, opinion never stated directly

Speaking Task ★★★

Target length: approx. 2 minutes.

  1. 1.
    Describe at least 3 features of the space, and explicitly explain why at least 2 of them matter, rather than just listing them.
  2. 2.
    Imagine your listener reacts with mild skepticism partway through. Describe how you would adjust your register or approach in response.
  3. 3.
    Use at least 2 different hedging phrases ("seems to," "tends to," "in a way") instead of flat statements of opinion.

Before you present, check:

  • 3+ features described, with significance explained for 2 or more
  • Register/approach adjustment clearly described
  • 2 or more different hedging phrases used naturally

Speaking Task ★★★★

Target length: approx. 2-3 minutes.

  1. 1.
    Deliberately select and emphasize at least 3 details to shape your listener's specific impression of the space.
  2. 2.
    Include at least 1 moment where you catch yourself using an imprecise word and repair it mid-delivery.
  3. 3.
    Use at least 1 piece of idiomatic vocabulary naturally in your description.
  4. 4.
    If you're describing a space you haven't personally visited, include a moment summarizing, in your own words, what someone else told you about it.

Before you present, check:

  • 3 or more details, clearly shaping a deliberate impression
  • Smooth, natural self-repair of an imprecise word
  • Idiomatic vocabulary used naturally, with register control
