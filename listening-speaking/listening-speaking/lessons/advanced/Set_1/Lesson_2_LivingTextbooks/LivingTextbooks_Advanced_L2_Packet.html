Good to Know

Restrictive covenants were clauses once written into many U.S. property deeds, including in Arizona, that barred certain groups, often Black families, from buying homes in a neighborhood. They are no longer legally enforceable, but the old wording still turns up in historic deeds.

"Mid-century modern" describes an architecture style popular roughly from the 1940s through the 1960s. Phoenix has an unusually large collection of it partly because so many soldiers trained at Arizona military bases during World War II and returned afterward to build new lives there.

Words to Know

1. preserve / preservationto protect something so it stays in its original condition
2. trovea valuable collection of things
3. mid-centuryfrom around the middle of a certain century (here, roughly 1945-1965)
4. registeran official list, here of buildings recognized and protected for their history
5. covenanta formal condition written into a legal document, like a property deed
6. beholdenhaving a duty or obligation toward someone or something

My grandfather's old fishing cabin sits on a lake two hours north of here. Small mid-century buildings like his hold a lot of local history, and the town recently put it on the local historic register because of that. Take my grandfather's cabin, for example: it's one of the last buildings left with the original covenant still in its deed, and inside it's still a small trove of old fishing gear and photographs from the 1950s. My uncle wasn't thrilled about the paperwork, but he says he feels beholden to preserve it anyway, for my grandmother's sake.

Learn the Phrase
Summarizing What Someone Said

The video opens with a line that compresses an entire community debate into its two core positions:

Real words from the video: "Some see older buildings as revered artifacts that have stood the test of time. Others see them as occupying sites that could be used for new development."

Say It Like a Native Speaker

Contrast makes a sentence land. Practice this real line from the video: "Some see older buildings as revered artifacts... others see them as occupying sites that could be used for new development." Stress "revered artifacts" and "new development" harder than the rest of the sentence, since the contrast between them is the whole point.
