Part A: TOEFL Listening Practice

Listen to an Academic Talk

Your teacher or a partner will read a short talk aloud once, from their own copy. You will not see the talk printed here and you will not be able to hear it again, just like the real TOEFL Listening section. Listen carefully, then answer the four questions below by choosing the best answer for each.

  1. 1.
    What is the talk mainly about?
    • A. The history of a small town in Maine
    • B. How a small-town restaurant became famous, and how its owner has tried to keep it feeling the same despite that growth
    • C. A specific recipe the restaurant is known for
    • D. How to start a small restaurant
  2. 2.
    According to the talk, how does a guest get a table at The Lost Kitchen?
    • A. Applicants submit their name for a reservation and are chosen at random
    • B. Tables are given on a first-come, first-served basis
    • C. Only local residents may reserve a table
    • D. Reservations open once a year for the whole season
  3. 3.
    What can be inferred about Erin French's approach to cooking?
    • A. She follows a fixed weekly menu
    • B. She imports ingredients from other countries
    • C. She prefers deciding what to cook based on what ingredients are available that day
    • D. She rarely changes her recipes
  4. 4.
    What is French's attitude toward her restaurant's growing popularity?
    • A. Entirely comfortable with rapid growth
    • B. Indifferent to the restaurant's popularity
    • C. Regretful that the restaurant became popular
    • D. Cautious: she wants the restaurant to succeed but worries about losing what makes it special
