Learn the Phrase
Checking What You Heard

In this video, Julia is not sure she understood. So she says the words back as a question. Sammy says, "that's my mother." Julia says, "That's your mother?" Later she says, "Your brother?" and "He's your nephew?!"

You can also ask "Who's that?" when you don't know a person.

  • • "Your brother?"
  • • "That's your mother?"
  • • "Who's that?"
