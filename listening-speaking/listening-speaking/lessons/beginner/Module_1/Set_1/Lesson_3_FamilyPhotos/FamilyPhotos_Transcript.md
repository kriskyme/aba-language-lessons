## Transcript (verbatim)

**Julia:** Have you got a message?

**Sammy:** Oh, it's a photo. Look!

**Julia:** She's pretty.

**Sammy:** Yes, she's beautiful!

**Julia:** And the boy. He, erm, looks like you.

**Sammy:** Yes, I think so. Isn't he handsome!

**Julia:** Yes, he's cute.

**Sammy:** Look! Here's another one. That's Gavin again. That's his name. He's two here. It's his
birthday and that's my mother.

**Julia:** That's your mother?

**Sammy:** Yeah. And, hang on. That's my father.

**Julia:** Your father? Hmm. With Gavin again?

**Sammy:** Yes. Everybody loves Gavin. He's so funny. Oh! Look at this one. Look! I love this photo.

**Julia:** Ah, Gavin again. ... Who's that?

**Sammy:** That's Neil. He's my brother.

**Julia:** Your brother?

**Sammy:** Yes.

**Julia:** And Neil is Gavin's uncle?

**Sammy:** Erm, yes, that's right.

**Julia:** Well, this is a surprise.

**Sammy:** Surprise? What?

**Julia:** Gavin!

**Sammy:** Gavin?

**Julia:** Your son?!

**Sammy:** Hmph. Gavin isn't my son, Julia!

**Julia:** But he's got the same hair, the same eyes ...

**Sammy:** Yes. That's because Gavin's my nephew.

**Julia:** He's your nephew?!

**Sammy:** Yes. He's my sister's son!

**Julia:** He's your sister's son. So the pretty woman in the photo is ...

**Sammy:** My sister Claire!

## Who is who (from the transcript itself)

- **Sammy** - the one showing the photos
- **Claire** - Sammy's sister; the "pretty woman" in the first photo
- **Gavin** - Claire's son, so Sammy's nephew; aged two in the birthday photo
- **Neil** - Sammy's brother, and therefore Gavin's uncle
- Sammy's **mother** and **father** each appear in one photo
