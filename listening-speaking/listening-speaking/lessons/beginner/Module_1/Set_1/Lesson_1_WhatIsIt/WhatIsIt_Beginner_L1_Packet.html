Unit 1B: What Is It?

Objective: name a familiar object out loud when someone asks "What is it?"

Learn the Phrase
Giving Examples

In the video, Anna's friends keep finding new things in her bag - each one is another example of "a thing in the bag." Practice giving examples the same way.

Use these frames:

  • • "Like what? A ___."
  • • "It is a ___."

Say It Like a Native Speaker

Questions go up at the end. Statements go down at the end. Practice: "What is it?" (voice goes up) "It is a book." (voice goes down).

Discuss It

Get into a small group. One person holds up a picture or a real object and asks "What is it?" The next person answers. Take turns. While your group talks, keep a tally: how many things did your group name?

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "A ___."
  • ★★ "It is a ___."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, pick one new object in the classroom (not book, pen, map, lamp, pillow, or bag). Ask "What is it?" and answer out loud.

  • ★★ "It is a ___."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___."
