Learn the Phrase
Saying When

In this conversation, every person says two things: what they are doing, and when. Anne says, "At 10 a.m. I am writing. Every day I do my morning show." Jonathan says, "When the studio light is on, I am recording my evening show."

Put the time first. The time word tells your listener when.

  • • "In the morning, I am ___."
  • • "At ___, I am ___."

Sound Focus

In English, the important words are louder and longer. The small words are quick and quiet. Listen to the line below, then underline the two strongest words. Listen again to check.

At 10 a.m. I am writing.

Now say your own line from the task above two times. Say it flat the first time. The second time, make the time word and the action word strong. Ask your partner which one was easier to follow.

Talk About Your Day

Your teacher gives your pair one time of day. Tell your partner what you really do then, and help each other say it with the frame. Then you'll get a new partner with a different time of day.
