**Lesson 2 generated 2026-09-10:** "Let's Learn English - Level 1 - Lesson 8: Are You Busy?" (VOA
Learning English) - see `Module_1/Set_1/Lesson_2_AreYouBusy/Lesson2_AreYouBusy.md`. The lesson anchors on

real speakers ("At 10 a.m. I am writing. Every day I do my morning show."). One deviation flagged: the
CSV's Level 2 and Level 3 frames are illustrated with an object example ("It is ___.") and are
instantiated here as person-and-time frames, since this lesson's approved topic direction is a routine
rather than a single object. Second lesson in this Set drawn from the same VOA Level 1 series - noted
in the lesson's self-check, since Section 0.2 names only three platform families at this Level.
Three cited Wikimedia Commons images embedded (`Set1_Beginner_Image_Credits.md`, new file).

**Lesson 3 generated 2026-09-10:** "Starting Out, Episode 09 - Family photos" (British Council
LearnEnglish) - see `Module_1/Set_1/Lesson_3_FamilyPhotos/Lesson3_FamilyPhotos.md`. **First lesson in
this Set from outside VOA's "Let's Learn English - Level 1"**, resolving the same-series repeat that
Lesson 2's self-check flagged, and the first with a British rather than American speaker. Its script was
confirmed three independent ways (a full one-fetch transcript, an independent second fetch returning
identical text, and the official support-pack PDF extracted locally, which also supplied the 2017 year).
Best clarification model in the Set: Julia's questions are genuine repair moves on a misunderstanding
("That's your mother?", "He's your nephew?!"). Three deviations flagged: **runtime is not stated by the
source and could not be verified** (estimated 1:30-2:00, teacher confirms on first playthrough); the
page's level field reads "A1 Elementary / A2 Pre-intermediate" and the delivery sits somewhat above the
Level 1 register ceiling, accepted deliberately in exchange for source variety; and the clip is two
adults in dialogue rather than the planned "child or narrator." Frames instantiated as relationship
frames, as in Lesson 2. Three cited CC0 images embedded.

**Lesson 4 generated 2026-09-10, completing Set 1:** "Let's Learn English - Level 1 - Lesson 14: How
About This?" (VOA Learning English) - see `Module_1/Set_1/Lesson_4_HowAboutThis/Lesson4_HowAboutThis.md`.
**This lesson's row was amended before generation.** Style Guide v2.27 §A.2 (2026-09-10) forbids any
item answered by reading a chromatic colour off a printed picture, which landed directly on the
planned "Colors & shapes" theme; the row was moved first to shapes and sizes, then to **sizes & fit**
once no A1 shapes clip with a verifiable transcript could be confirmed (Oxford's shapes song is
JavaScript-gated and returns an empty page body, so its lyrics exist only in a search index; PBS's is
a well-fitting 1:57 but prints no transcript and exposes no caption file - both rejected under
Section 0.3's never-select-an-unverifiable-source rule). Three deviations flagged: **~5:00 runtime**
against the 30 sec-2 min Level 1 target, kept because the register is squarely on target and this was
the only verifiable source on the topic; the **topic narrowed twice** from the approved row; and this
is the **third clip from the same VOA Level 1 series** (Lessons 1 and 2 used its Lessons 4 and 8),
which Lesson 3 had broken with British Council. Pronunciation is contrastive stress, the fourth
distinct feature in four lessons. Three cited museum images embedded, told apart by outline alone so
they survive grayscale. **Set 1 is now complete (4 of 4 lessons).**
