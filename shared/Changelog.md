| 2026-09-10 | v1.17 (Quality Standards) | **Commit, verify against the source, then return unsupported.** Per user question about listening pedagogy ("what's the research on providing the listening transcript. so maybe first time they listen without it, then secodn time they listen with it"). The instinct is well supported and the program had no rule for it at all: nothing in `shared/` covered how many times a student meets the anchor input, or whether they ever get to see the input's own wording. Listening/Speaking's Day 1 played the clip exactly once and locked the transcript teacher-side, so a student who misheard a word boundary had no way to find out why; its Phase 5 produce-then-reveal shape ran on an invented script, not on the real source. The research is consistent that a transcript works as feedback on a hypothesis already committed to, not as a co-present channel (Diao, Chandler & Sweller 2007 found simultaneous transcript plus audio underperformed audio alone; Vandergrift & Goh's 2012 metacognitive sequence puts the transcript last; Montero Perez et al. 2013 on captions carries the same dependency caveat), and that the final exposure should be unsupported so the form-to-sound mapping consolidates in the ear. New **§D12** states the three-beat arc (Commit, Verify, Return unsupported), forbids folding Verify into Commit, names each lesson type's own form of it, and exempts assessments, where a verification beat would contaminate the measure. New §F item 26 matches. Swap test passed on the arc, not on the mechanics: the transcript window is Listening-only, but "commit, then check against the source, then meet it again unsupported" swaps to Passage Reading (locate the evidence line after committing a `[STOP & CHECK]` answer) and to Academic Writing (compare a draft against the Mentor form, then produce one fresh instance without it). Only Listening/Speaking's mechanics were built this pass, at the user's direction; Reading's and Writing's forms of the beat are logged by name in their own `Index.md` Pending work so §D12 does not quietly become a one-modality rule. Prompts swept: no prompt in any modality restated this rule, because none existed, so no prompt was bumped for §D12 itself; Listening/Speaking's Lesson and Student Packet prompts were bumped for the mechanics (see that lesson type's `Changelog.md`). Its Assessment prompts were deliberately left alone under the exemption. Generated content: needs-new-research branch, since choosing a window means reading each transcript and picking a stretch, so zero lessons were swept and all 23 are named by path in Listening/Speaking's `Index.md`. |
