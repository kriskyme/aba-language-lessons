Use these words when you need them. You may also use your own.

How to Spell a Word from Its Sounds
Four steps
1. Say the word.
2. Stretch it slowly and count the sounds on your fingers.
3. Write a letter or a letter pair for each sound.
4. Read it back. Does it say the word you meant?

Share a Line

Your teacher will read three models from the board. Find the one at your own star level, then say to a partner one thing the next model up does that yours does not.

  • ★"My model is ___ book. The next one up also has ___."
  • ★★"My model is a whole sentence. The next one up also has ___."
  • ★★★"My model has two words that say ___ and ___."

While you listen: say back the one difference your partner names before you say yours.
