Two Words That Say Two Different Things
The rule
The word and sits between the two blanks. The two words must describe two different things about the object.
It is black and soft. (a color and a feel: two things)
It is thin and slim. (a size and a size: one thing, said twice)
It is red and lined. (a color and what is on the cover: two things)
