Unit 4A: Describing My Hat

Writing · Describing Beginner 1.1.4.0

Objective: I can describe something of my own by choosing the word that really fits it and spelling that word from its sounds.

Words for a Hat
Color: red · black · tan · gray · blue
Size and shape: big · flat · long · round · wide
Feel: soft · warm · thick · smooth · rough
Condition: new · old · worn · clean · damp
Cover and marks: plain · striped · spotted · checked · bright

Writing about a hat for the first time today? Circle one you own: baseball cap · beanie · sun hat · wool hat · bucket hat · rain hat · visor · headband.

The Frames
★ ___ hat
★★ It is ___.
★★★ It is ___ and ___.
What Your Reader Wants

Task A. Trade pages with a partner and read their line. Write on their page one part of the hat you would like a second line about, from this list: the top · the brim · the color · the inside · the band · the front.

  1. 1.The part I would like to hear about:
Trade and Check

Task B. Your partner reads their line aloud again. Mark this form on their page.

  1. 1.I could say what each word describes. (yes / no)
  2. 2.The word tells me something I could not guess about the hat. (yes / no)
  3. 3.The word I liked best:

Task C. ★★★ Mark one more line on your partner's page.

  1. 1.The two words told me two different things. (yes / no)
One More Line

Write your frame one more time, about the part your reader asked for. Then read both of your lines and circle the one that tells a reader more about your hat. Leave the other one on the page.

Task D. ★ Write your new phrase, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1. hat
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.

Task E. ★★ Write your new sentence, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1.It is .
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.

Task F. ★★★ Write your new sentence with two words that say two different things, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1.It is and .
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.
Share a Line

Read out both of your lines and say which one you circled.

  • ★"I circled ___ hat, because it says ___."
  • ★★"I circled It is ___, because it says ___."
  • ★★★"I circled It is ___ and ___, because those two words say ___ and ___."

While you listen: put a mark next to the word on your own page that your neighbour's choice makes you want to change.

Unit 4B: Describing My Hat

Writing · Describing Beginner 1.1.4.0

Objective: I can describe something of my own by choosing the word that really fits it and spelling that word from its sounds.

Final Copy

Task A. Write a clean copy of the line you circled, word for word. Two stars and three stars: capital at the start, period at the end.

Read It Out

Your writing is finished. Read your final copy aloud to one partner, then to a second partner. Stay in your seat; the pages move, not the class.

  • ★Read your phrase, then say the name of your hat.
  • ★★Read your sentence.
  • ★★★Read your sentence, then say which two things your two words describe.

While you listen: say back the one thing the line tells you about the hat, in your own words, before you read yours.

Wrap It Up

Now do the same thing for something new: your own shoe. Work with a partner and write on a small card. Your partner reads your card and says what your word describes.

Task B. ★ Write your phrase on the card, then say the sounds of your word aloud in order.

Task C. ★★ Write your sentence on the card, with a capital and a period.

Task D. ★★★ Write your sentence on the card with two words that say two different things.

  1. 1.It is and .
