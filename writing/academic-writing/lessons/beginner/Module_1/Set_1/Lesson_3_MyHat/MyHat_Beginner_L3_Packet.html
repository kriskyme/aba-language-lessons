Unit 3B: Describing My Hat

Writing · Describing Beginner 1.1.3.0

Objective: I can describe something of my own by choosing the word that really fits it and spelling that word from its sounds.

Words for a Hat
Color: red · black · tan · gray · blue
Size and shape: big · flat · long · round · wide
Feel: soft · warm · thick · smooth · rough
Condition: new · old · worn · clean · damp
Cover and marks: plain · striped · spotted · checked · bright
A More Exact Word
nice · soft, warm, smooth
big · wide, long, round
old · worn, faded, damp
good · clean, bright, thick
dark · black, gray, blue
Check and Improve Your Writing

Anything you change: cross out the old word with a single line, do not erase it, and write the new word above it, so both stay on the page.

Task A. ★ Check your phrase, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your phrase to a partner. Your partner says what your word describes.
  2. 2.Could your partner say what it describes? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Does your word have a letter or a letter pair for every sound you hear? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does your word say something true about your own hat? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Is there a more exact word above that fits? If yes, cross out your old word.
  6. 6.Write your phrase again. If your word was already the best one, leave it and put a check next to it: hat
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.

Task B. ★★ Check your sentence, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your sentence to a partner. Your partner says what your word describes.
  2. 2.Could your partner say what it describes? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Does your word have a letter or a letter pair for every sound you hear? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does your sentence start with a capital and end with a period? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Is there a more exact word above that fits? If yes, cross out your old word.
  6. 6.Write the whole sentence again: It is .
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.

Task C. ★★★ Check your sentence, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your sentence to a partner. Your partner says what each word describes.
  2. 2.Did your partner say two different things back, not the same thing twice? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Do your two words come from two different groups in the list above? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does your sentence start with a capital and end with a period? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Cross out the word your partner found hardest to picture, and write a more exact one above it.
  6. 6.Write the whole sentence again: It is and .
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.
Read It to Someone New

Read your improved line to a different partner. Your partner says the one word they can picture best, and one thing that word tells them about the hat. Nothing is rewritten now.
