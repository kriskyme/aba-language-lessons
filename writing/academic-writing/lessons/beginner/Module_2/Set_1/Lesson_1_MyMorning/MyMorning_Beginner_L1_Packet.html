Adding -ed
What it does
A verb names what you did. Adding -ed says it already happened: I walk (now) becomes I walked (before now).
RuleWhenExample
1. Add -edmost verbswalk, walked; wash, washed; look, looked
2. Add -dthe verb already ends in eclose, closed; smile, smiled; taste, tasted
3. Double the last letter, then add -edone short vowel and one consonant at the endhop, hopped; zip, zipped; step, stepped
4. Change y to i, then add -eda consonant before the yhurry, hurried; tidy, tidied; carry, carried
The Three Sounds of -ed
/t/ walked, packed, washed, hopped
/d/ filled, smiled, closed, hurried
/id/ waited, tasted, needed, counted
The Frames
★ First, ___.
★★ I ___-ed.
★★★ First, I ___-ed. Then, I ___-ed.

A two-star and three-star sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a period.
