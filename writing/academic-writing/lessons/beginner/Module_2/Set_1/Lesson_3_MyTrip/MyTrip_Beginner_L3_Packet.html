A More Exact Verb
moved · walked, rushed, climbed
used it · packed, zipped, locked
looked · watched, checked, noticed
helped · carried, tidied, counted

Check and Improve Your Writing

Anything you change: cross out the old word with a single line, do not erase it, and write the new word above it, so both stay on the page.

Task A. ★ Check your line, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your line to a partner. Your partner says what happened.
  2. 2.Could your partner say what happened? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Does your word have a letter or a letter pair for every sound you hear? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does your word name something that really happened on your own trip? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Is there a more exact word above that fits? If yes, cross out your old word.
  6. 6.Write your line again. If your word was already the best one, leave it and put a check next to it: First, .
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.

Task B. ★★ Check your sentence, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your sentence to a partner. Your partner says what happened.
  2. 2.Could your partner say what happened? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Does your verb use the right one of the four rules for -ed? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does your sentence start with a capital and end with a period? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Is there a more exact verb above that fits? If yes, cross out your old verb.
  6. 6.Write the whole sentence again: I .
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.

Task C. ★★★ Check your story, then make it better.

  1. 1.Read your story to a partner. Your partner says what happened and which action came first.
  2. 2.Did your partner say the same action came first that you meant? (yes / no)
  3. 3.Do your two verbs name two different actions, not one action twice? (yes / no)
  4. 4.Does each verb use the right rule for -ed, with a capital and a period on both sentences? (yes / no)
  5. 5.Cross out the verb your partner found hardest to picture, and write a more exact one above it.
  6. 6.Write the whole story again: First, I . Then, I .
  7. 7.Read it to your partner.
Read It to Someone New

Read your improved line to a different partner. Your partner says the part they can picture best, and whether it happened at the start, the middle, or the end of the trip. Nothing is rewritten now.
