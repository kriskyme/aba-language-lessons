Unit 4A: My Trip

Writing · Narrating Beginner 2.1.4.0

Objective: I can tell a reader what happened, in the order it happened, and spell each word from its sounds.

Words for a Trip
Event words: bus · walk · line · stop · rain · shop · game · snack
Getting-ready verbs: packed · dressed · zipped · locked · tidied
Moving verbs: walked · rushed · stepped · hurried · climbed
Doing-there verbs: shopped · looked · picked · counted · carried
Waiting and looking verbs: waited · checked · watched · smiled · noticed
Time words: first · then · next · after that · last

Writing about a trip for the first time today? Circle one you have really taken: to the store · to school · to the park · to a friend's house · on the bus · to the market · to a game · to the library.

The Frames
★ First, ___.
★★ I ___-ed.
★★★ First, I ___-ed. Then, I ___-ed.
What Your Reader Wants

Task A. Trade pages with a partner and read their line. Write on their page one moment of the trip you would like a second line about, from this list: before you left · the way there · the waiting · the middle · the way back · right at the end.

  1. 1.The moment I would like to hear about:
Trade and Check

Task B. Your partner reads their line aloud again. Mark this form on their page.

  1. 1.I could say what happened. (yes / no)
  2. 2.It tells me something about the trip I could not have guessed. (yes / no)
  3. 3.The word I liked best:
One More Line

Write your line one more time, about the moment your reader asked for. Then read both of your lines and circle the one that tells a reader more about your trip. Leave the other one on the page.

Task C. ★ Write your new line, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1.First, .
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.

Task D. ★★ Write your new sentence, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1.I .
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.

Task E. ★★★ Write your new story with two verbs that name two different actions, then circle the better of your two.

  1. 1.First, I . Then, I .
  2. 2.Say to a partner why you circled that one.
Share a Line

Read out both of your lines and say which one you circled.

  • ★"I circled First, ___, because it says ___."
  • ★★"I circled I ___-ed, because it says ___."
  • ★★★"I circled First, I ___-ed. Then, I ___-ed, because it says what happened first and what happened after."

While you listen: put a mark next to the word on your own page that your neighbour's choice makes you want to change.

Unit 4B: My Trip

Writing · Narrating Beginner 2.1.4.0

Objective: I can tell a reader what happened, in the order it happened, and spell each word from its sounds.

Final Copy

Task A. Write a clean copy of the line you circled, word for word. Two stars and three stars: capital at the start, period at the end.

Read It Out

Your writing is finished. Read your final copy aloud to one partner, then to a second partner. Stay in your seat; the pages move, not the class.

  • ★Read your line, then say which trip it was.
  • ★★Read your line.
  • ★★★Read your line, then say which action happened first.

While you listen: say back what happened, in your own words, before you read yours.

Wrap It Up

Now do the same thing for something new: last night. Work with a partner and write on a small card. Your partner reads your card and says what happened.
