The Time Word Goes First
The rule
First, I washed.
Then, I packed my bag.
Next, I walked to the bus.
Last, I waited.
The time word comes first, then a comma, then what you did. First and last are the two ends. Then, next, and after that move the story along in the middle.
This is not an order: First, I walked. First, I waited. (Two firsts. A reader cannot tell which came first.)
