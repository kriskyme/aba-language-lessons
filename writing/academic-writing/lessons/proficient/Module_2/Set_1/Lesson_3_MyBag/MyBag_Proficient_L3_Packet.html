Before You Plan

What you are writing today

At ★: a second paragraph telling one real thing that happened with your bag, in order, with why it mattered and one detail that lets a reader guess how you feel.

At ★★, ★★★, and ★★★★: the introduction and the conclusion of your piece, around the body paragraphs you already have. Nobody rewrites the part they already have.

The order inside each part

The introduction runs hook, then two or three connecting sentences that narrow, then the thesis last. Your working thesis is copied out unchanged; it is not rewritten.

The conclusion says the thesis again in new words, then makes one closing move: a suggestion, a prediction, a question, or an opinion. It adds no new information.

Check and Improve Your Writing

Lay both parts in front of you, the part you already had above the part you wrote yesterday. Mark on the page: circle, underline, box. Cross out rather than erase.

Task A. ★ Check both paragraphs, then improve them.

  1. 1.In the first paragraph, circle one shortened clause. Does it have its commas right: none if it says which one you mean, two if it adds information about a thing already named?
  2. 2.In the first paragraph, box the detail that lets a reader guess how you feel. Is it still a fact rather than an adjective about you?
  3. 3.In the second paragraph, underline every verb. Is the line of events in the past simple, and does every reach-back use had plus the past participle?
  4. 4.Circle each cue word you used. Are there at least two?
  5. 5.Underline the sentence that says why it mattered.
  6. 6.Box the detail that lets a reader guess how you feel here. Is there a feeling word anywhere in the paragraph? If there is, it has to go.
  7. 7.Read both in order. Do they describe the same bag without contradicting each other, and does the second have 6 to 9 sentences?
  8. 8.Replace one flat word with a more exact one from the Draft Word Bank.
  9. 9.If a reach-back is doing its work with the verb form alone, add a cue word in front of it. If your two boxed details imply the same thing, change one.
