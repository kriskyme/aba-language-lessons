## DAY 1: PEER EDITING AND REVISION (75 MIN)

**Peer Editing Form (all task Levels, Level-specific questions marked):**

1. (Level 4) Circle the comparative word your partner used, and underline the "because" reason. Does the reason
   make sense as a real reason?
2. (Level 5) Circle one hedge word your partner used. What does it suggest they feel, without saying it
   outright?
3. Put a box around one concession or compound sentence. Is the comma in the right place?
4. (Levels 6-7) Without being told, can you identify the hook, the thesis, and each body paragraph's topic
   sentence? If not, tell your partner where you got lost.
5. (Level 6) Find the sentence that talks directly to Sam about Sam's concern. Does the register genuinely
   shift there?
6. (Level 7) What do you think the writer is implying but never states? Check it against the writer's own
   self-revision note: does it match, or did the revision aim somewhere else?
7. One specific compliment:
8. One specific suggestion:

**Revision time:** students revise based on their checklist pass and partner feedback. **Level 7 students must
produce one of the concrete self-revision artifacts from Section 0.4a**; this is not optional at Level 7. Levels
4-6 are encouraged to do the same if time allows.

Quick revision share: a before/after board built from two or three volunteered examples, most naturally from
Level 7.

---

## DAY 2: PUBLISHING AND CLOSING TRANSFER CHECK (75 MIN)

Final polish/proofread pass against the Day 2 (Lesson 3) checklist. Publish/share: a short gallery walk or
read-aloud; everyone reads or hears 2-3 classmates' work.

**Closing Transfer Check:** in pairs, every student applies the spotlighted skill to something new: "In two or
three sentences, compare two different things, hint at how you feel about one of them without saying it
outright, and give a real reason for the comparison, written for a specific reader with a specific concern."
Level 6-7 pairs are additionally asked to state, out loud, what a one-sentence direct or indirect thesis for that
new comparison would be. The teacher cold-calls 2-3 pairs to share; this is not a self-report. Board-dependent
moment (Section 0.7): capture instances live on the board.
