## DAY 1: DRAFTING, PART 1 (75 MIN)

**Levels 4-5:** draft the opening and body of their paragraph, per Section 0.2's targets (Level 4: 40-90 words,
3-6 sentences; Level 5: 90-150 words, 6-9 sentences), using Lesson 2's prewriting.

**Levels 6-7:** draft the introduction and the first body paragraph. It is fine to finish only the first body
paragraph today; Day 2 completes the rest.

Mid-draft share-out: a few students from each task Level read one sentence or one paragraph. Board-dependent
moment (Section 0.7): a running "strong sentence/paragraph" board.

---

## DAY 2: DRAFTING, PART 2 AND SELF-EDIT (75 MIN)

**Levels 4-5:** complete the paragraph, meeting their Section 0.2 target.

**Levels 6-7:** draft the remaining body paragraph(s) and the conclusion.

**Self-edit checklist pass, Levels 4-7 (shared base, Level-specific items marked):**

- Every sentence has a subject and a verb; tense is consistent throughout.
- (Level 4) At least one comparative structure (-er or more) is used correctly, and "because" gives one clear,
  explicit reason.
- (Level 5) At least one hedge word (seems, tends to, a bit, at least, worth it, admittedly) is used to imply an
  attitude, with no feeling word stated directly in that sentence.
- At least one concession sentence (although, even though) or compound sentence (and/but/so) is used correctly,
  with a comma in the right place.
- "your" and "you're" are used correctly wherever they appear.
- (Levels 6-7) The essay has a hook, and it is a real hook, not a flat statement of the thesis.
- (Levels 6-7) The thesis is present at the end of the introduction and is the correct kind (direct at 6,
  indirect at 7).
- (Levels 6-7) Every body paragraph opens with a topic sentence stating that paragraph's own point.
- (Levels 6-7) The conclusion restates the thesis and introduces no new information.
- (Level 6) The essay explicitly names why one feature matters to Sam, and shifts register at least once.
- (Level 7) The essay embeds an implication a reader must infer, and the self-revision evidence (0.4a) is
  present and visible.
- Punctuation and capitalization are correct.

Share-out / final line check: a quick read-aloud or partner check of the closing sentence/paragraph.
Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7): add to Day 1's board with newly completed lines.
