Check Your Own Work

Every sentence has a subject and a verb; tense is consistent throughout.

★ At least one comparative structure (-er or more) is used correctly, and "because" gives one clear, explicit reason.

★★ At least one softening word (seems, tends to, a bit, at least, worth it, admittedly) is used to hint at a feeling, with no feeling word stated directly in that sentence.

At least one downside sentence (although, even though) or compound sentence (and/but/so) is used correctly, with a comma in the right place.

"your" and "you're" are used correctly wherever they appear.

★★★★ The essay has a hook, and it is a real hook, not a flat statement of the thesis.

★★★★ The thesis appears at the end of the introduction, and is the right kind for your star level (direct at three stars, indirect at four).

★★★★ Every body paragraph opens with a topic sentence stating that paragraph's own point.

★★★★ The conclusion restates the thesis and introduces no new information.

★★★ The essay explicitly names why one feature matters to Sam, and shifts its language at least once to speak directly to Sam.

★★★★ The essay lets the reader infer one idea you never state directly, and shows a visible crossed-out-and-replaced revision.

Punctuation and capitalization are correct.
