Wrap It Up

Sit with a partner. Think of your own desk or table at home, and ask your partner two questions about theirs. Write on your card, then read it to your partner.

Task E. ★ Compare your desk to your partner's.

  1. 1.Write one sentence comparing your desk with your partner's, with a reason word (because, since, as, or given that) and a real reason. Example: My desk is smaller than Ana's, because it has to fit under the window.

Task F. ★★ Compare your desk to your partner's, then add a detail.

  1. 1.Write one sentence comparing your desk with your partner's, with a reason word and a real reason.
  2. 2.Write a second sentence with one real detail about your own desk that lets your partner guess how you feel about it, with no feeling word.
  3. 3.Your partner reads it and says the guess out loud. If the guess is wrong, write a sharper detail.

Task G. ★★★ Write a three-sentence mini-paragraph for your partner.

  1. 1.A topic sentence stating one point about your desk compared with your partner's.
  2. 2.One supporting sentence.
  3. 3.One sentence that says why that point would matter to your partner, using their name.

Task H. ★★★★ Write a three-sentence mini-paragraph with a concession.

  1. 1.A topic sentence stating one point about your desk compared with your partner's.
  2. 2.One supporting sentence, and make one of your three sentences a concession (although, even though, while).
  3. 3.Choose your detail so that your partner infers how you feel about your own desk without being told. Your partner says what they inferred.
