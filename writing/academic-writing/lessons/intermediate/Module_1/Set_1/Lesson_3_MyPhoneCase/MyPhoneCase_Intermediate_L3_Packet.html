Unit 3A: Describing My Phone Case

Writing Intermediate S1.3.0

Objective: You will describe your phone case by comparing it to something else and giving a real reason for the comparison. Some of you will also describe it in a way that lets your reader guess how you feel about it, without ever saying the feeling out loud.

Look back at Lesson 1's examples and your own Lesson 2 planning before you begin.

Task A. ★ Using your own actual phone case, complete "It is ___." with one word you sound out yourself.

Task B. ★★ Using your own actual phone case, complete "It is ___ and ___." with two words you sound out yourself.

Task C. ★★★ Draft your description: one comparison, one explicit "because" reason, and at least one compound sentence. Aim for about 40-90 words in 3-6 sentences.

Task D. ★★★★ Draft an extended description, organizing your details logically and building in the one detail that hints at how you feel. No sentence should state the feeling word itself ("I love this case" fails the task; a specific, well-chosen detail succeeds). Aim for about 90-150 words in 6-9 sentences.
