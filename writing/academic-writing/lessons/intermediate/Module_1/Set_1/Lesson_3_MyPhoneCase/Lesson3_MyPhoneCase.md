## DAY 1: DRAFTING, PART 1 (75 MIN)

```
0      10                              60         75  (minutes)
|Phase 1|------------Phase 2------------|-Phase 3--|
| Mentor| Independent/guided drafting:   | Mid-draft |
| re-look| opening and body               | share-out |
```

### Phase 1: Mentor Ladder and prewriting re-look (10 min)

Each student re-reads their own task Level's Mentor Text (Lesson 1) and their Lesson 2 prewriting notes.

### Phase 2: Independent/guided drafting, opening and body (50 min)

**Levels 2-3:** complete the real frame(s) for their own actual object (not the practice object from Lesson 2):
Level 2 writes "It is \_\_\_." with one sounded-out word; Level 3 writes "It is \_\_\_ and \_\_\_." with two sounded-out
words. The teacher circulates and listens to each student sound out their word choice before it goes on the page.

**Level 4:** draft an original description including one comparison, one explicit "because" reason, and at least
one compound sentence, using Lesson 2's prewriting as a starting point rather than a copy-in. It is fine to
finish only part of the draft today; Day 2 completes it.

**Level 5:** draft an extended description, organizing details logically and building in the one implied-attitude
detail circled in Lesson 2. Remind students directly: no sentence should state the feeling word itself ("I love
this case" fails the task; a specific, well-chosen detail succeeds).

Teacher circulation should specifically check that the required feature is present in-progress.

### Phase 3: Mid-draft share-out (15 min)

A few students from each task Level read one sentence from their draft so far. Board-dependent moment (Section
0.7): a running "strong sentence" board, populated from the share-out.

## DAY 2: DRAFTING, PART 2 AND SELF-EDIT (75 MIN)

```
0        30                        55                      75  (minutes)
|--Phase 1--|---------Phase 2--------|--------Phase 3---------|
| Complete  | Self-edit checklist    | Share-out / final       |
| the draft | pass                   | line check              |
```

### Phase 1: Complete the draft (30 min)

Students finish their piece, meeting their own task Level's full Section 0.2 target.

### Phase 2: Self-edit checklist pass (25 min)

**Levels 4-5 checklist:**

- Every sentence has a subject and a verb; third-person singular verbs end in -s where needed.
- I used at least one comparative structure (-er or more) correctly.
- I used "because" to give one clear, explicit reason.
- I used at least one compound sentence (and/but/so) with a comma before the connector.
- (Level 5 only) I included one specific, concrete detail that shows how I feel without any feeling word
  ("love," "like," "hate") appearing in that sentence.
- "it's" and "its" are used correctly wherever they appear.
- Punctuation and capitalization are correct.
  **Levels 2-3 equivalent (oral, not written):** read your sentence to a partner. Does your word match the picture
  or your real object? Does the sentence make sense out loud?

### Phase 3: Share-out / final line check (20 min)

A quick read-aloud or partner check of the piece's closing sentence. Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7):
return to and add to Day 1's "strong sentence" board with newly completed lines.
