## Grammar Focus B (this lesson's centerpiece, Levels 4-5)

**Focus B (this lesson centerpiece, Levels 4-5):** simple versus compound sentences (and/but/so), including comma
placement before the connector. This is Level 4's paired sentence-variety point, matching the Scenario's own
comparison-plus-reason content naturally.

**Level 5 extension (folded into this lesson's close and Day 2):** hedging/detail-selection language, plus one
concession subordinator (although, even though) as an optional upgrade from a plain "but" compound sentence.

**Confusable pair (this lesson):** "it's" vs. "its," a high-frequency error in object description at this band.

### Phase 1: Focus B mini-lesson: simple and compound sentences (20 min)

**Simple sentences** have one subject-verb combination (or more than one subject and/or verb, still one
sentence). **Compound sentences** join two simple sentences with a comma and a connector: and, but, or so.

| Connector | Meaning          | Example                                 |
| --------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------|
| and       | adds information | My case is blue, and it is thin.        |
| but       | adds contrast    | My case is thin, but it is heavy.       |
| so        | gives a result   | My case cracked, so I bought a new one. |

Tie it explicitly to Focus A: "Yesterday you learned to compare two things and give a reason. Today we'll join
that comparison to its reason, or its result, in one sentence using a compound sentence."

Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7), co-constructed live: elicit one fresh compound sentence about a new object
in the room (not the phone case), built from student call-outs.

### Phase 2: Controlled practice B (30 min)

**Activity 6: Identify each sentence as simple (S) or compound (CD); add the missing comma where needed
(whole class, 12 min).**

> 1. My case is blue and thin.
> 2. My case is blue, and my sister's case is red.
> 3. I like this case, but I want a different color.
> 4. I picked it up and put it in my bag.
> 5. It cracked, so I bought a new one.
> 6. I use it at home and I use it at work.
> 7. I like this case, but my sister likes hers better.
> 8. It's thin and it's easy to carry.

**Activity 7, Levels 4-5: Combine each pair of sentences into one compound sentence, choosing and, but, or so
(12 min).**

> 1. I bring my phone case with me every day. It protects my phone from drops.
> 2. I chose this color. It doesn't show fingerprints as easily.
> 3. My old case cracked. I bought a new one.
> 4. This case is more expensive. It lasts much longer.
> 5. I clean it every morning. I wipe it again at night.
> 6. My sister wanted a bright case. I wanted a plain one.

**Activity 7-equivalent, Levels 2-3 (parallel, 12 min):** a light coordination note, then guided practice: the
two-slot frame "It is \_\_\_ and \_\_\_." already uses "and" to join two words, not two full sentences; point this out
explicitly, then have students complete two more rounds of the two-slot frame on the picture, pulling both words
from the Word Bank and saying each completed sentence aloud.

### Phase 3: Confusable pair and mixed editing (25 min)

**Confusable pair: "it's" vs. "its."**

| Form | Meaning                                     | Example                                     |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------|
| it's | it is / it has                              | It's blue. It's been my case for two years. |
| its  | belonging to it (possessive, no apostrophe) | Its corner is cracked.                      |

**Activity 8: Choose it's or its (whole class, 8 min).**

> 1. **\_\_\_** my favorite phone case.
> 2. **\_\_\_** corner is a little worn.
> 3. **\_\_\_** been dropped more times than I can count.
> 4. I like **\_\_\_** color the best.
> 5. **\_\_\_** heavier than it looks.
> 6. My case lost **\_\_\_** shine after a year.

**Activity 9: Mixed editing, 8 errors across Focus A and Focus B (Levels 4-5, 17 min).**

> My phone case is more good than my old one, it hold my phone better. I like it because it are light so I bring
> it everywhere. Its heavier than my old case but that don't bother me. My sister's case are smaller, and she
> think it easyer to carry.

Verified errors (8): "more good" -> "better"; comma splice after "old one" -> period or semicolon before "it";
"it hold" -> "it holds"; "it are light" -> "it is light"; "Its heavier" -> "It's heavier"; "that don't bother" ->
"that doesn't bother"; "case are smaller" -> "case is smaller"; "she think it easyer" -> "she thinks it's
easier."

### Phase 1: Frame practice round 2 / required-feature warm-up (25 min)

**Levels 2-3:** three more guided practice rounds directly on the phone case Scenario, each choosing a different
pair of observations from the Word Bank, building toward Lesson 3's real frame completion.

**Levels 4-5: Activity 10.** Write two comparison sentences and two because-sentences (not yet combined) about
their own phone case, then join one comparison-reason pair into a compound sentence using this lesson's Focus B
pattern. Level 5 also writes two sentences, each containing a concrete, specific detail (not a feeling word),
then trades with a partner and asks "what does this detail make you think I feel about my case?" for both.

### Phase 2: Prewriting for the Scenario, differentiated by task Level (30 min)

**Levels 2-3:** circle 2-3 words on the Word Bank they plan to use (color, size, one more descriptive word) as
their own shortlist to draw from in Lesson 3.

**Level 4:** a short two-column prewriting note: "What I'm comparing my case to" / "My real reason," plus one
planned compound sentence joining the two.

**Level 5:** a short prewriting list of 3-4 concrete, specific details about their own phone case (not feeling
words), then circle the one detail they think most clearly implies something about how they feel, to build the
Lesson 3 draft around.

### Phase 3: Prewriting share and board synthesis (20 min)

A few students from each task Level share one word or detail. Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7): build a
shared class word/detail bank on the board, in two columns (Description words / Detail ideas), adding to the
Word Bank rather than replacing it.
