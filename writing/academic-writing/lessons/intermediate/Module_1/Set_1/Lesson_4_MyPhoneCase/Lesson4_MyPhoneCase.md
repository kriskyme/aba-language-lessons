## DAY 1: PEER EDITING AND REVISION (75 MIN)

```
0            25                              60         75  (minutes)
|--Phase 1----|--------------Phase 2------------|-Phase 3--|
| Peer Editing | Revision time                   | Quick     |
| exchange     |                                  | revision  |
|              |                                  | share     |
```

### Phase 1: Peer Editing exchange (25 min)

**Levels 4-5 Peer Editing Form:**

1. Circle the comparative word your partner used. Does it clearly compare two things?
2. Underline the "because" reason. Does it make sense as a real reason, not just a restatement?
3. Put a box around one compound sentence. Is the comma in the right place?
4. (Level 5 partners only) Without asking the writer, what do you think they feel about their phone case? What
   one detail told you that?
5. One specific compliment:
6. One specific suggestion:
   **Levels 2-3 equivalent:** an oral partner check. Read your sentence aloud to your partner. Can your partner
   point to the phone case picture and then to the word you used?

### Phase 2: Revision time, including self-revision evidence (35 min)

Students revise based on their checklist pass and their partner's feedback. This band tops out at Level 5, so the
hard Levels 7-8 self-revision requirement in Section 0.4a does not apply; Level 5 students are recommended (not
required) to use the visible strikethrough method: cross out (not erase) the sentence or detail they are changing
and write the replacement next to it, so both versions stay visible. Levels 4 and below can revise with a clean
rewrite if time is short.

### Phase 3: Quick revision share (15 min)

Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7): a before/after board built from two or three volunteered revision
examples, most naturally from Level 5 students who used the visible-revision method.

## DAY 2: PUBLISHING AND CLOSING TRANSFER CHECK (75 MIN)

```
0        20                        50                      75  (minutes)
|--Phase 1--|---------Phase 2---------|--------Phase 3---------|
| Final     | Publish/share            | Closing Transfer        |
| polish    |                          | Check                   |
```

### Phase 1: Final polish / proofread pass (20 min)

A last quiet read-through against Lesson 3's checklist, focused on anything Day 1's revision changed.

### Phase 2: Publish/share (30 min)

A short gallery walk. Students post their phone case description (with the picture, if Level 2-3) around the
room; everyone reads 2-3 classmates' work.

### Phase 3: Closing Transfer Check (25 min)

In pairs, every student applies the spotlighted skill to something new: "In one sentence, describe something else
in this room by comparing it to something else, with a real reason." Levels 4-5 add the reason and, where
relevant, one implied detail; Level 4-5 pairs are also encouraged to join their sentence into a compound sentence
if it fits naturally. The teacher cold-calls 2-3 pairs to share aloud. Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7):
capture 2-3 of these instances live on the board as students share.
