## DAY 1: SELF-REVISION AND DRAFT COMPLETION (75 MIN)

```
0            25                              60         75  (minutes)
|--Phase 1----|--------------Phase 2------------|-Phase 3--|
| Self-edit    | Self-revision time              | Quick     |
| checklist    |                                  | revision  |
| pass         |                                  | share     |
```

### Phase 1: Self-edit checklist pass (25 min)

**Levels 4-5 checklist:**

1. Circle your own comparative word. Does it clearly compare two things?
2. Underline your "because" reason. Does it make sense as a real reason, not just a restatement?
3. Put a box around one compound sentence. Is the comma in the right place?
4. (Level 5 only) Without telling a partner, can they guess what you feel about your phone case just from reading
   it? What one detail would tell them that?
   **Levels 2-3 equivalent:** an oral self-check. Read your sentence aloud. Does it match the phone case picture
   and the word you chose?

### Phase 2: Self-revision time, including self-revision evidence (35 min)

Students revise based on their own checklist pass and complete the draft to full length. **No Peer Editing
exchange at this position** - that happens once, at Module 2's Lesson 4, once Module 2's own tools are also
available (see the note above). This band tops out at Level 5, so the hard Levels 7-8 self-revision requirement
in Section 0.4a does not apply; Level 5 students are recommended (not required) to use the visible strikethrough
method: cross out (not erase) the sentence or detail they are changing and write the replacement next to it, so
both versions stay visible. Levels 4 and below can revise with a clean rewrite if time is short.

### Phase 3: Quick revision share (15 min)

Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7): a before/after board built from two or three volunteered revision
examples, most naturally from Level 5 students who used the visible-revision method.

## DAY 2: DRAFT HAND-OFF AND CLOSING TRANSFER CHECK (75 MIN)

```
0        20                        50                      75  (minutes)
|--Phase 1--|---------Phase 2---------|--------Phase 3---------|
| Final     | Hand-off note            | Closing Transfer        |
| polish    |                          | Check                   |
```

### Phase 1: Final polish / proofread pass (20 min)

A last quiet read-through against Lesson 3's checklist, focused on anything Day 1's revision changed, completing
the draft to full length.

### Phase 2: Hand-off note, not publishing (30 min)

Students write a short note (a sentence, or a labeled picture for Levels 2-3) naming what Module 1's own focus
(comparatives, because-reasons, compound sentences) added to their description, and that it now moves to Module
2's Set for revision, peer editing, and publishing. **Do not publish or share the piece at this position** - no
gallery walk, no posting around the room.

### Phase 3: Closing Transfer Check (25 min)

In pairs, every student applies the spotlighted skill to something new: "In one sentence, describe something else
in this room by comparing it to something else, with a real reason." Levels 4-5 add the reason and, where
relevant, one implied detail; Level 4-5 pairs are also encouraged to join their sentence into a compound sentence
if it fits naturally. The teacher cold-calls 2-3 pairs to share aloud. Board-dependent moment (Section 0.7):
capture 2-3 of these instances live on the board as students share.
