A Reader's Request

Task A. Trade pieces with a partner and read.

  1. 1.Read your partner's piece all the way through.
  2. 2.On the last line of their page, write one thing you want to know more about ("I want to know more about ___"; for a one-line piece, "Tell me about the ___").
  3. 3.Give it back.
Make It Stronger

Anything you change: cross out the old version with a single line, do not erase it, and write the new version next to it or above it, so both stay on the page.

Task B. ★ Answer your reader, then choose your best.

  1. 1.Find the part your reader asked about (a sleeve, a pocket, the collar, the hood).
  2. 2.Write one more sentence about that part, with a word you spell by its sounds: It is .
  3. 3.Read both sentences (this one and the one from before) to a partner.
  4. 4.Circle the one that says the most about it. That is the one you will copy next time.

Task C. ★★ Answer your reader, then choose your best.

  1. 1.Find the part your reader asked about (a sleeve, a pocket, the collar, the hood).
  2. 2.Write one more sentence about that part, with two words that say two different things: It is and .
  3. 3.Read both sentences (this one and the one from before) to a partner.
  4. 4.Circle the one that says the most about it. That is the one you will copy next time.

Task D. ★★★ Make three changes that make your paragraph stronger.

  1. 1.Find two short sentences that belong together. Join them with and, but, or so, with a comma before the connector. Cross out the old two and write the new one.
  2. 2.Find one plain word (big, nice, good, old). Cross it out and write a more exact word from the Word Bank or your own (faded, baggy, sturdy).
  3. 3.Add the sentence your reader asked for, with a real detail in it. Stay inside 6 sentences: if you now have 7, join two or cut the weakest.

Wrap It Up

Sit with a partner. Put the pen or pencil you are each holding side by side. Write on your card, then read it to your partner.

Task E. ★ Write "It is ___." about your partner's pen, with a word you spell by its sounds (long, short, blue, thin).

Task F. ★★ Write "It is ___ and ___." about your partner's pen, with two words that say two different things.

Task G. ★★★ Compare your pen to your partner's.

  1. 1.Write one sentence comparing your pen to your partner's, with a reason word (because, since, as, or given that) and a real reason. Example: My pen is thinner than Ana's, because I like to keep it in my pocket.
  2. 2.If it fits, join the comparison and the reason with and, but, or so.

Task H. ★★★★ Compare your pen to your partner's, then add a detail.

  1. 1.Write one sentence comparing your pen to your partner's, with a reason word and a real reason.
  2. 2.Write a second sentence with one real detail about your own pen that lets your partner guess how you feel about it, with no feeling word.
  3. 3.Your partner reads it and says the guess out loud.
  4. 4.If the guess is wrong, write a sharper detail.
