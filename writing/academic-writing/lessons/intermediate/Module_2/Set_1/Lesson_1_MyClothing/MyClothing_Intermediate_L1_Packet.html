Objective: I can tell what happened with something of my own, in order, and say how it turned out and why.

Word Bank

Wearing verbs: zipped · buttoned · tied · wrapped · tucked · stretched
Getting verbs: picked · wanted · needed · ordered · carried · saved
Time words: first · then · next · after that · finally · last winter · one morning
People and places: my mother · my friend · the store · the market · school · the bus · the rain
Grammar

Telling What Happened (Past Tense)

RuleExamples
Most verbs: add -edwash → washed, fold → folded, want → wanted
One vowel + one consonant at the end: double the consonant, add -eddrop → dropped, zip → zipped, plan → planned
Consonant + y at the end: change y to i, add -edtry → tried, carry → carried, dry → dried
Verb already ends in -e: add -d onlytie → tied, save → saved, order → ordered (no double r: two vowels come before it)

Watch the two rules people break most: drop becomes dropped (not droped), and try becomes tried (not tryed). The -ed ending can sound like /t/ (washed), /d/ (tied), or /id/ (wanted), but it is always written -ed.

Verbs That Do Not Take -ed

wear → wore, get → got, buy → bought, choose → chose, put → put, take → took, find → found, lose → lost, give → gave, see → saw, pay → paid, is / are → was / were

Time Words

JobWordsExample
Put events in orderfirst, then, next, after that, finallyFirst I tried on the black hoodie. Then I saw the gray one.
Say when it happenedlast winter, one morning, yesterday, on the first dayLast winter I bought this scarf at the market.
CommaA short opening time phrase can go with or without a comma. In this piece, write it without one.Last winter I bought this scarf. / One morning I dropped it on the bus.

A story needs one word that says when it started and at least two words that keep the events in order. The sentence that says how it turned out uses a reason word: because, since, as, or given that. I still wear it because it has never torn.

Use the frame "I ___-ed it." with a verb from the Word Bank, and spell the -ed ending as part of the verb. For "I ___-ed it and ___-ed it.", pick two verbs that name two different actions, in the order they happened (washed it and folded it, not washed it and cleaned it).
