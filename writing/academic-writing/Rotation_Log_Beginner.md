# Academic Writing Rotation Log - Beginner Band

Nested by Module and Set, one row per lesson, matching Reading's and Listening/Speaking's own Rotation Log shape
now that Academic Writing uses the same 2-day-lesson/4-lessons-per-Set model (`shared/Program_Conventions.md`
§C, corrected 2026-09-08 from an original 8-day/1-lesson-per-Set model). Created 2026-09-10, on this Band's
first Set. See `Rotation_Log.md` for the cross-Band check to run before planning a new Set.

Beginner is exempt from Module Pairs (`shared/Program_Conventions.md` §C), so this Band's rows carry no Pair
position: a Set is one Module's own 4 lessons, ending in a finished, published piece at Lesson 4.

## Module 1: Describing

**Grammar Focus (A/B):** Focus A: letter-sound spelling patterns for describing words (short-vowel CVC plus
common blends and digraphs: red, tan, big, wet, flat, thin, black, soft, slim, plain) and the sound-it-out
routine itself, Level 1's row, with Level 2's own row (the fixed one-slot sentence frame "It is ___.") folded in
/ Focus B: "and" joining two different describing words inside the two-slot frame "It is ___ and ___." (Level
3's row, the only Focus B the frame regime offers); confusable pair the short e / i vowel spelling (red/rid,
ten/tin, wet/wit, bed/bid, pen/pin, beg/big). Essay Focus: N/A, this Band never reaches Levels 6-8.

**Real-world writing form:** descriptive writing about an object (Module 1's row), realized at frame scale as a
one-sentence description of the student's own object, not a paragraph.

**Notes: three decisions recorded at plan time.** (1) **Focus row anchoring.** Lesson prompt 0.4's row-selection
rule anchors Focus A/B at a band's native Paragraph Composition row and names only Intermediate, Advanced, and
Proficient; Beginner has no Paragraph Composition Level, so the rule does not resolve. This Set anchors at Level
1's row, the Band's calibration Level (Conventions §A). Logged as a prompt gap in `Index.md`'s Pending work.
(2) **The simple-present -s form is not taught in this Set**, a consequence of that anchoring, so Lesson 3 Day
2 runs only the swap-in-a-more-exact-word half of its phase and adds no second sentence. (3) **Cross-Band
recency flag, raised and not blocked:** "hat" is one of the eight items in Intermediate Module 1 Set 1's
clothing draft list. Answered by making this Band's list hat-specific (eight kinds of hat, not eight kinds of
clothing); per Conventions §F a topic echo across Bands is flagged, not blocked.

**Cross-Band check run at plan time (Conventions §F; this Band's first Set, so no cross-Set or cross-Module
check applies).** Against Proficient Module 1 Set 1 (2026-09-10, generated in a parallel pass the same day and
re-checked against before this Set was logged: practice object my headphones, draft object my bag, Focus A/B
hand-selected off Level 5's row, confusable pair lie / lay), Intermediate Module 2 Set 1 (2026-09-09), and
Advanced Module 1 Set 1 (2026-09-08): no collision on the Focus pair (every logged focus so far is composition-regime

no collision on the writing form (the three prior Sets produced paragraphs and essays; this one produces a

on "hat," recorded above.


## Module 2: Narrating

### Set 1 (My Morning, then My Trip; planned 2026-09-10 from `Module2_Beginner_Lesson_Plan.md` 1.0, generated against Lesson prompt v6.17 and packet prompt v3.17)

All 4 lessons share one Scenario and one Grammar Focus A/B pairing; Lessons 1-2 write about the practice event
and Lessons 3-4 about the draft event (Conventions §C). Beginner is Pair-exempt, so this Set is self-contained
and carries nothing from Module 1: a new Scenario, a new Focus pair, and its own published piece at Lesson 4.

| # | Lesson # | Set position | Date | Content role | Scenario / Topic | Version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Beginner 1 | 1 of 4 | 2026-09-10 | Grammar Focus A (the regular past -ed: four spelling rules, three -ed sounds) input, modeling, deeper practice; Skill Spotlight; Mentor Ladder (teacher-presented, 3 Mentor Texts); holds the Set's one guided frame round | My morning (practice event) | 2.1.1.0 |
| 2 | Beginner 2 | 2 of 4 | 2026-09-10 | Grammar Focus B (time-order words at the front of a sentence); doubling-rule confusable drill; Day 2 partner dictation, then prewriting introduces the draft event and the Draft Word Bank | My morning; then my trip introduced Day 2 (draft event: one of to the store, to school, to the park, to a friend's house, on the bus, to the market, to a game, to the library) | 2.1.2.0 |
| 3 | Beginner 3 | 3 of 4 | 2026-09-10 | Drafting on the draft event: the frame completed once and complete on Day 1; check and improve on Day 2 | My trip (draft event) | 2.1.3.0 |
| 4 | Beginner 4 | 4 of 4 | 2026-09-10 | Finale: a reader's request and Peer Editing, one more frame sentence and the better of the two chosen; final copy, publishing, Closing Transfer Check on last night | My trip (same Scenario, continued) | 2.1.4.0 |

**Grammar Focus (A/B):** Focus A: the regular past `-ed` - its four spelling rules (add -ed: walk, walked; add -d
after e: like, liked; double the final consonant after a short vowel: hop, hopped; consonant plus y becomes ied:
hurry, hurried) and its three sounds (/t/ in walked and packed, /d/ in filled and listened, /id/ in waited and
needed), hand-selected for Module 2 at Level 1's row, with Level 2's own row (the fixed one-slot past frame "I
___-ed.") folded in / Focus B: time-order words (first, then, next, after that, last) at the front of a sentence
with the comma after them, marking which action came first, from Level 1's own Module 2 row; confusable pair the
doubling rule (hoped/hopped, taped/tapped, planed/planned, pined/pinned). Frames: "First, ___." (Level 1, an
event word), "I ___-ed." (Level 2), "First, I ___-ed. Then, I ___-ed." (Level 3, a two-sentence sequence). Essay
Focus: N/A, this Band never reaches Levels 6-8.

**Real-world writing form:** personal narrative / journal entry (Module 2's row), realized at frame scale as a
captioned event or a one-or-two-sentence sequence about something the student really did, not a paragraph.

**Notes: four decisions recorded at plan time.** (1) **Focus row anchoring, again unresolved by the prompt.**
0.4's row-selection rule still names only Intermediate, Advanced, and Proficient, so this Set anchors at Level
1's row as Module 1 did (Conventions §A). (2) **A second gap in the same bank, new to this Module:** 0.4's
Grammar Focus Bank rows for Levels 1-3 are written for Describing (Level 3's Focus A cell is the simple-present
-s form; its Focus B cell is "and" inside a two-slot frame), neither of which fits a Narrating Set, so Focus A/B
here is hand-selected against Module 2's own CSV rows. Logged in `Index.md`'s Pending work. (3) **No irregular
verbs anywhere in the Set**, so no Word Bank, model, or item contradicts the -ed rule being taught; irregulars
belong to a later Set. (4) **Three 0.4d slots deliberately rotated** away from Module 1's order (pair judgment to
Lesson 1 Day 1, better of two to Lesson 1 Day 2, fix-the-wrong-word and sort to Lesson 2 Day 1), so a class
coming straight off Module 1 does not meet the identical sequence of task shapes twice.

**Cross-Module and cross-Band checks run at plan time (Conventions §F).** Against Beginner Module 1 Set 1
(2026-09-10, the binding cross-Module check): no repeat on the Focus pair, the confusable pair, the frames, the
events, or the writing form. Against Proficient Module 1 Set 1 (2026-09-10), Intermediate Module 2 Set 1
(2026-09-09), and Advanced Module 1 Set 1 (2026-09-08): the doubling pair is new (e/i, it's/its, then/than,
quiet/quite, and lie/lay are spent), and no Band has used a morning or a trip as its Scenario. **Three flags,
raised and not blocking.** (a) Intermediate Module 2 Set 1 is also a Narrating Set whose Focus A names the -ed
spelling rules; this is fixed by the CSV, since Module 2's Level 2 row is literally "I ___-ed.", and the only
alternative (anchoring on letter-sound spelling off Level 1's row) would repeat Beginner Module 1's own Focus A,
a stronger same-Band adjacency. What differs: this Set adds the three -ed sounds and teaches no irregulars, where
Intermediate's centerpiece was a twelve-verb irregular list and a connector range at paragraph scale. (b) The
frames are deliberately not Intermediate's "I ___-ed it." and "I ___-ed it and ___-ed it." (c) "My morning"
echoes Intermediate Module 2's Closing Transfer Check prompt "this morning," a single card task in another Band;
this Set's own Transfer Check uses last night.
