Note: Beginner's task Levels are 1, 2, 3 - three, not four (`shared/Program_Conventions.md` §B) - and all three
Module 1 Reading objectives end in matching a decoded word or frame to an image, so every lesson in this Set
needs real Wikimedia Commons images embedded at generation time under Conventions §I and rowed into
`lessons/beginner/Module_1/Set_1/Set1_Beginner_Image_Credits.md`. See
`lessons/beginner/Module_1/Module1_Beginner_Lesson_Plan.md` for the per-lesson image list, the §0.11
shape-to-slot assignment, and the Closing Transfer Check object per lesson; the credits for the six
photographs used by Lessons 1 and 2 are in `lessons/beginner/Module_1/Set_1/Set1_Beginner_Image_Credits.md`.

Note (2026-09-10, Lessons 1 and 2): both anchors are calibrated to Level 1, which Section 0.4 leaves below
the idiom requirement, so neither lesson carries an Idiom/Slang Spotlight and neither packet carries a
Phrase Spotlight. Every picture task turns on size, shape, or weather rather than colour, because the
packets print in black and white.


Note (2026-09-10, Lessons 3 and 4): Lesson 4's planned theme word *ear* was replaced by *long*, because
Level 3's two-slot frame needs a second describing word the photographs settle and the notice names its ears
by colour rather than by a word of their own; the notice still describes both ears. Lesson 4 is also the only
lesson in this Set whose items turn on colour, which `shared/Student_Packet_Style_Guide.md` §A.2 permits for
black and white alone, since those survive a black-and-white print.
