Notes on this Set:

- **This Band's anchor is a paired text set, not a single passage.** Level 7 is Proficient's anchor Level and its
  objective requires two extended descriptions of the same subject, so every lesson in this Set is built on two
  texts of roughly 330-360 words, lettered as one continuous sequence ([A]-[D] and [E]-[H]). Levels 5 and 6 work
  the first text only; Levels 7 and 8 work the pair. The Genre column above therefore names a pairing.
- **No retired formation appears anywhere in this Set.** The hooks and protocols were chosen in their
  room-neutral forms from the start (Quality Standards §D11), unlike the Intermediate and Advanced Set 1 rows,
  which still carry Fishbowl, Concentric Circles, Four-Corner Debate, and K-W-L Walk pending rename.
- All four lessons generated 2026-09-10 against `Generate_Lesson_Prompt_v3.5.md`, in two passes (1-2, then
  3-4). Lesson 1 embeds one Commons photo (see `Set1_Proficient_Image_Credits.md`); Lessons 2-4 need none,
  their hooks being a printed quotation, a printed marked statement, and a printed chart.
- **Board-moment slots rotate across the Set** (Section 0.7): Lesson 1 Day 1 Phase 1 + Day 2 Phase 2, Lesson
  2 Day 1 Phase 1 + Day 2 Phase 3, Lesson 3 Day 1 Phase 2 + Day 2 Phase 2, Lesson 4 Day 1 Phase 1 + Day 2
  Phases 1 and 2. Lessons 3 and 4 are the two that carry a board record from one day into the next: Lesson
  3's tone map gains a second row on Day 2, and Lesson 4's K-W-L chart has its L column closed on Day 2.
- **Closing Transfer Check subjects, none repeated** (Quality Standards §D3): today's shoes, a drink you buy
  regularly, the last room you slept in, the weather outside right now. Each is described twice, from two
  positions, which is this Set's shared skill.
- Day 1 Phase 3's native-Level item set is a framing set in all four lessons, not the "Cause & Effect set"
  the Lesson prompt names, which would drift a Describing lesson into Explaining (§A4). See `Index.md`'s
  Pending work for the prompt fix this is waiting on.
