Unit 4A: Lost Cat

Reading · Describing Beginner 1.1.4.0

Objective: I can sound out a word that tells how an animal is marked, and find the animal it describes.

Before You Read

Here is one line from something you have not read yet.

Her fur is very soft to touch.

As a class, say who you think her is, and where you would read a line like this. Your teacher will write your ideas on the board and check them after you read.

Words to Know

6. longit goes a long way from one end to the other

Discuss It

First, get into a group that works on one part of the notice: Pip's markings, Pip's tail, or Pip's ears. Agree one sentence about your part. Then make new groups of three, one person from each part, and say your sentence to your new group. Your new group will get one question below, and will swap for a second one later. Use a starter phrase from the list.

1. Someone tells you they found a cat. Say one thing about Pip they should check.
2. Think of an animal you know. Say two things about how it looks.
3. Which is easier to find outside at night, a black cat or a white cat? Say why.

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "A black ___ ."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, think of your own phone. Say how it is marked, using a starter phrase from the list.

  • ★ "A black phone."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
