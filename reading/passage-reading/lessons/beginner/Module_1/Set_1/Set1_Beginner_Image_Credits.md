# Passage Reading - Beginner Module 1 Set 1 - Image Credits

Every image embedded in this Set's student packets, one table per lesson. All are Wikimedia Commons files,
fetched and cited at generation time per `shared/Program_Conventions.md` §I; each lesson `.md` carries the same
citation at the point of use, and this document is where the credits are published. Packets print no credit
(`shared/Student_Packet_Style_Guide.md` §F). Asset files live in the named lesson's own folder.

Every Beginner Reading lesson needs embedded photographs: all three Module 1 Reading objectives end in matching
a decoded word or frame to an image, and `shared/Generation_Quality_Standards.md` §D8 rules out anything the
teacher would have to prepare. The pictures are chosen so the match turns on size, shape, or weather rather than
colour, since the packet prints in black and white.

Lesson 4 is the only lesson in this Set whose items turn on colour. That is the case
`shared/Student_Packet_Style_Guide.md` §A.2 permits: black and white survive a black-and-white print, so a
black cat, a white cat, and a black-and-white cat stay distinguishable on the page. Lessons 1 to 3 use size,
shape, and weather instead.
