At the Bus Stop

Tom waits at the bus stop. The seat is wet from the rain. His hands are cold. He has a hat, and the hat is dry. Then the sun comes out, and the day gets hot.

Work in a group of three, with one person on each task below. Do your own task first, then say your answer out loud to your group and put one answer on the board.

Discuss It

Get into a small group. Your group will get one question below, and will swap for a second one later. Take turns answering out loud, using a starter phrase from the list.

1. What is the weather like today? Tell your group two things about it.
2. Ana likes a hot day at the sea. Which do you like, a hot day or a wet day? Say why.
3. What do you wear on a hot day? What do you wear on a cold day?

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "A hot day."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, think of your own water bottle. Say how it feels, using a starter phrase from the list.

  • ★ "A cold bottle."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
