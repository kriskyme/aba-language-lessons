[A] Nina and Raj stop at a fruit stand. The Seller stands behind the fruit.

[F] Raj: And I take the long banana, please. We can eat them at school today.

Discuss It

Some of you will speak in turn from your seat while the rest listen. While you listen, write down three describing words a speaker uses well. You can use your list when it is your turn. Use a starter phrase from the list.

1. Which fruit would you buy at this stand? Say what it is like.
2. Nina says the pineapple is too big for her bag. What fruit fits in your bag? Say why.
3. Think of a fruit that is not on this stand. Say two things about it.

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "A small ___ ."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, think of your own jacket. Say how big it is and what shape it is, using a starter phrase from the list.

  • ★ "A long jacket."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
