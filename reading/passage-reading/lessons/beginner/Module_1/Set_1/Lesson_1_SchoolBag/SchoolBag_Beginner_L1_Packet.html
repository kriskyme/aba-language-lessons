At the Shop

Mum goes to the shop on Saturday. She gets a big bag for the food. She gets a pen for Sam. The pen is small and long. She gets a new book for Sam too.

Task B
Read the story again and do both steps.

  1. 1.
    Circle these six words in the story as you find them: bag, book, pen, big, small, long.
  2. 2.

Discuss It

Talk with a partner. Find a new partner for each question below, and use a starter phrase from the list.

1. What is in your school bag? Tell your partner two things.
2. Which thing in your bag is big? Which one is small?
3. Which thing do you use every day? Say what it is like.

Starter phrases:

  • ★ "A big ___ ."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
Wrap It Up

With a partner, think of your own pencil case. Say how big it is, using a starter phrase from the list.

  • ★ "A small case."
  • ★★ "It is ___ ."
  • ★★★ "It is ___ and ___ ."
