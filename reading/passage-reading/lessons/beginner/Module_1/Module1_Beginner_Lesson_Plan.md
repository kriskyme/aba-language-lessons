## Task Levels for this plan (Beginner Band)

Per Conventions §B, Beginner's task Levels are **1, 2, 3** - native 1 and 2 plus one extension up to 3, and no
extension down, because Level 1 is the floor of the whole scale. That is **3 task Levels, not 4**: every lesson
below carries exactly three differentiated task-Level sections, and the packets print ★ / ★★ / ★★★ with no
★★★★ anywhere. The anchor text itself is calibrated to **Level 1**, the band's lower Level (Conventions §A):
60-120 words, one memorized frame, concrete high-frequency decodable vocabulary, 6-10 word sentences, literal
picture-or-object match with no inference (Lesson prompt §0.2).

Quoted verbatim from `learningobjectives.csv`, Module 1: Describing, **Reading modality only**:

| Level | Reading Can-do |
|---|---|
| 1 | "Can decode an unfamiliar simple word or short adjective-noun phrase (e.g. sounding out "big," "red," "hat") using known letter-sound patterns, and match it to the object or image it describes, with visual support." |
| 2 | "Can read one short sentence using the fixed frame ("It is ___.") describing a familiar object, decoding the open-slot word using known letter-sound patterns to identify the matching image, with visual support." |
| 3 | "Can read one short sentence using the two-slot frame describing a familiar object, decoding both open-slot words using known letter-sound patterns to identify the matching image." |

**Note on colour (added 2026-09-10, Style Guide v2.27 §A.2).** No item may be answered by reading a chromatic
colour off a printed picture, because every packet prints in grayscale. Build picture items on size, shape,
quantity, texture, pattern, or setting instead. Black, white, and grey are fine, which is why Lesson 4's
black-and-white cat still works; Lesson 3's theme was moved off colour onto size and shape for this reason.
Colour may still appear in an anchor text or a vocabulary list, just not as what an item is answered with.

**Note for Step 2 (lesson generation).** All three objectives end in matching a decoded word or frame to an
**image**. Quality Standards §D8 forbids any teacher-prepared media, and its order of preference lands on option
(3) here - a picture task cannot be carried by the student's own belonging or an object in the room, because the
student must choose between two or more printed candidates. So every lesson in this Set needs real images
fetched from Wikimedia Commons at generation time under Conventions §I, embedded in the packet as base64
data-URIs, named at the point of use in the lesson `.md`, saved per §H, and rowed into
`Set_1/Set1_Beginner_Image_Credits.md`. The packet is not generated until its images exist; no placeholder box
and no "the picture your teacher shows you" ever appears. The last column of the plan table names what each
lesson needs.

Task formats favor variety across oral, collaborative, and visual formats; writing is one format among several,
never the default, and no task below is grounded in a Writing CSV row even where the student writes a word.

## Topic grounding

The Module Lesson-Plan prompt grounds topics by band: **Beginner may use a generic, familiar scene**, and this
plan takes that option throughout. The reason is the Level 1 ceiling above - a real, verifiable referent at this
band drags in proper nouns and lower-frequency vocabulary that fight a 60-120 word text built from decodable
simple-phonics words. The four scenes are a school bag, a sunny day at the beach, a fruit stand, and a lost cat.
No topic names a real individual, and nothing is attributed as a quote to a real person.

## Fixed-output shape and slot assignment (§0.11 / Quality Standards §D10)

Levels 1 and 2 have fixed output forms, and Level 3's two-slot frame is equally fixed, so all three are planned
against the §0.11 shape bank. The prompt requires the Set's plan to name the slot-to-shape assignment so no
shape lands in the same slot in consecutive lessons:

| Slot | Lesson 1 | Lesson 2 | Lesson 3 | Lesson 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 Phase 1 (pre-teach) | Sort first | - | Sort first | - |
| Day 1 Phase 3 (Fact Finder) | Decode and match | Choose what fits | Decode and match | Choose what fits |
| Day 2 Phase 1 (refresher task) | - | Fix the wrong word | - | Fix the wrong word |
| Day 2 Phase 2 (matrix cell) | Better of two | Decode and match | Read to a partner | Decode and match |
| Day 2 Phase 3 | Transfer | Transfer | Transfer | Transfer |

No shape occupies the same slot in two consecutive lessons. Transfer is exempt from that check: it is the
Closing Transfer Check, required in every lesson by Quality Standards §D3 and oral-only by §0.8, so it is not a
written production and does not count against §0.11's once-per-day cap on producing the form. Each lesson's one
written production is therefore its single Decode and match item.

**Closing Transfer Check objects,** one fresh object per lesson and none repeated across the Set (§D3), each the
student's own belonging described from what they know of it - never handled, held up, taken out, or pointed at

## Self-check

- **Genre variety.** The Beginner bank (Lesson prompt §0.6) names label sets with embedded images, short notices,
  short personal notes/texts/postcards, simple 2-4 line dialogues, and very short how-to lists - five forms. This
  Set samples four, one per lesson, no repeats and no two consecutive lessons alike. The how-to list is left
  unused for a later Set; it was the weakest fit for the Describing verb.
- **Reading-strategy variety.** Four distinct strategies, one per lesson (Teacher Read-Aloud with Interactive
  Stops, Echo Reading, Reader's Theater, Partner/Shared Reading), past the "at least 3 distinct" floor with no
  consecutive repeat. Close Reading with Annotation is deliberately not used: §0.6 reserves it for Advanced and
  Proficient independent readers.
- **Hook rotation.** Visual Inquiry, K-W-L Chart, Take a Side, Mystery Quote - each used exactly once across the
  four lessons.
- **Protocol rotation.** Rotating Partners, small-group discussion, Panel Round, Jigsaw Expert Panels - four of
  the five §0.10 protocols, each once. Lesson 3's Panel Round carries an explicit listener task (a running list
  of three describing words that struck the listener as well put, per §0.10's Describing option), and the lowest
  task Level's version of that list doubles as its differentiated participation under §0.8 rather than being a
  second requirement. Every lesson runs 2-3 rotated prompts advanced at each changeover, never one static
  question.
- **Room-neutral (Quality Standards §D11).** Every hook and protocol above runs from students' seats. No
  Fishbowl, Concentric Circles, Four-Corner Debate, K-W-L Walk, carousel, gallery walk, corners, or stations
  appears anywhere in this plan, and nothing is posted around the room.
- **Topic and vocabulary overlap.** All four topics and all four vocabulary themes are distinct within the Set,
  and none echoes the Intermediate or Advanced Module 1 themes already logged. No earlier Set exists in this
  Module/Band to check against.
- **Topic grounding.** All four are generic familiar scenes, which the prompt's topic rule permits at Beginner;
  the reason is recorded under Topic grounding above. No fabricated quote is attributed to a real, named
  individual.
- **Task-Level grounding.** Every task-Level entry traces to the verbatim **Reading** row quoted above - L1's
  decode-and-match-to-image, L2's one-slot frame plus image identification, L3's two-slot frame plus image
  identification - applied to that lesson's own scene. Exactly three task Levels per lesson, matching the
  Beginner Band. No Writing or Listening/Speaking row grounds any task.
- **Fixed-output shapes.** The slot table above assigns a shape per slot per lesson with no shape in the same
  slot in consecutive lessons, and exactly one written production per lesson day.
- **Module-verb consistency.** Every task stays inside Describing - features, sizes, shapes, markings, and the
  frame sentences that name them. None drifts into Explaining (why something happens), Evaluating (a verdict), or
  Instructing (a procedure), which is also why the how-to genre was left out of this Set.
- **Lesson numbering.** This Band had no prior lessons, so the global numbering starts at 1: Lessons 1-4, version
  codes `1.1.1.0` through `1.1.4.0` (Conventions §G).
- **Cross-Set / cross-Module adjacency.** Skipped, per Conventions §F, as this Band's first Set.
