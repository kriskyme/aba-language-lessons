False-colour ASTER satellite image of the southern entrance to the Suez Canal, in the Gulf of Suez, taken on
4 April 2021, showing more than a hundred ships waiting at anchor after the canal reopened. Chosen over
photographs of the ship itself because it withholds the cause, which is what the Visual Inquiry hook turns on.
