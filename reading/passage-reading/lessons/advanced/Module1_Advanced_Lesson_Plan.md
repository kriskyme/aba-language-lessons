## Task Levels for this plan (Advanced band)
 
Per Section 0.1 of the v2.5 lesson prompt's Task Levels by Band table, Advanced's task Levels are **4, 5, 6, 7**
(extension-down: 4; native: 5, 6; extension-up: 7) - 4 task Levels, so every lesson below carries 4 differentiated
task-Level sections. The anchor text itself is calibrated to Level 5 (Advanced's lower/native level, 400-460
words, B2), per Section 0.1's "anchor text is calibrated to the band's LOWER level" rule. Quoted verbatim from
`learningobjectives.csv` (Module 1: Describing), **Reading modality only**:
 
| Level | Reading |
|---|---|
| 4 | "Can read a short descriptive text about a familiar object and identify its features, one comparison, and the brief reason given for that comparison, decoding unfamiliar words as needed using known letter-sound patterns." |
| 5 | "Can read an extended description, summarize how the writer organizes details, and identify at least one clearly evaluative word choice that signals the writer's attitude even though the writer never states the opinion directly, decoding unfamiliar words as needed." |
| 6 | "Can read an extended description and identify specific word choices that create a positive, negative, or neutral impression, separating explicitly stated description from clearly-signaled evaluative comments, and locate at least one place where the writer's language visibly shifts partway through (e.g. from confident claims to a hedge like 'results may vary')." |
| 7 | "Can read two extended descriptions of the same subject, critically assess how each writer's rhetorical choices shape a different impression, and identify at least one unstated interest or assumption behind one writer's framing that isn't explicitly acknowledged, using sophisticated inference and register awareness." |
 
**Note for Step 2 (lesson generation):** Level 7's objective genuinely requires *two* texts, unlike Intermediate's
extension-up Level 5, which only needed the single shared anchor text. Each lesson below will need a short
second comparison text at generation time for the Level 7 task only (the other three Levels still work from the
one shared anchor). I've suggested a natural second-text angle per lesson in the Task-Level Basis column
(typically an official/promotional framing vs. an independent one) - not mandatory, just a starting point.
 
Task formats favor variety - oral, collaborative, visual/physical - rather than defaulting to writing; where writing
shows up (e.g. in Homework), it's a format choice, not something traced to a Writing Can-do objective.
 
## Topic grounding
 
Per the Module Lesson-Plan prompt's topic rule, Advanced (well above Intermediate) leans further into genuinely
real-world subject matter. All 8 topics below name a real, documented place, craft, or practice. The surrounding
frame (a journal entry, an interview, a social post) is still invented, and no quote or statement is attributed to a
real, named, identifiable individual - Lessons 2, 4, 6, and 8 use fictional/composite narrators (an apprentice, a
lighthouse keeper, a reviewer, social-media posters) describing a real subject, never a real specific person's
actual words; Lesson 7 attributes statements to "city officials" generically rather than a named real person.
These are grounded in stable, well-established real-world knowledge, not a live search this pass - a live search
is worth running later if a more current example is wanted for a specific class.
