# Set 1 Intermediate Study Guide — Task Level 3 (★★)

Companion to `Set1_Intermediate_Assessment.md`. Review this guide for about 30 minutes right before the test.
This guide does not use the test's new passages or repeat any test item — it reviews the words and skill you
will need, using different examples.

## Vocabulary & Idiom Review

**Lesson 1: A Grandmother's Kitchen**
- **warmth** — a comfortable heat. Example: *"Maria says her candles bring warmth to any room, even without a
  flame."*
*(Lesson 1: A Grandmother's Kitchen)*

**Lesson 2: New Corner of Yoyogi Park Opens to Visitors**
- **renovated** — repaired or rebuilt to look new again. Example: *"The city library finished a small renovation
  of its front entrance last week."*
- **"before long"** — soon, after a short time. Example: *"Before long, the new benches were full of families
  enjoying the view."*
*(Lesson 2: New Corner of Yoyogi Park Opens to Visitors)*

**Lesson 3: How to Choose Running Shoes That Feel Comfortable**
- **cushioning** — soft padding that protects your foot. Example: *"Look for padding, or cushioning, on the
  straps and along your back."*
*(Lesson 3: How to Choose Running Shoes That Feel Comfortable)*

**Lesson 4: Painting Wynwood Walls: An Interview**
- **spray paint** — paint that comes out of a can as a fine mist. Example: *"I painted it last spring using spray
  paint and a few brushes for the fine lines."*
*(Lesson 4: Painting Wynwood Walls: An Interview)*

## Skill Review

The skill every lesson in this Set practiced: **describing something by comparing it to something else, using
specific evidence from the text.**

- **Lesson 1:** the grandmother's kitchen uses a wood-fired oven; her own explanation compares it directly to a
  modern electric oven and gives a reason (a smokier, better flavor) for preferring it.
- **Lesson 2:** the article compares the park's new stone path to the old dirt path, with a specific reason
  (the new path stays dry) for why the change matters.
- **Lesson 3:** the guide compares a shoe that fits well in the store to one that only seems to fit, and gives a
  reason (it will not "stretch out" later) for trusting the in-store fit.
- **Lesson 4:** the artist compares this year's bird mural to last year's wave mural, and gives a reason (she
  wanted this wall to feel like motion, not calm) for the different color and shape choices.

## Sample Items (worked examples — not on the test)

**Short-answer-with-evidence example:** *According to the "At the Market" refresher text, why do some
customers say Maria's candles remind them of their own grandmother's kitchen?* A strong answer gives the
specific detail the text states (the herbs give the candles a soft, natural aroma), not a guess.

**Quote-the-exact-phrase example:** *What exact phrase in "A New Look for the Library Entrance" tells you the
new path solves the old rain problem?* Answer: *"the new path also stays dry after rain, which was a common
complaint before."* A full-credit answer quotes the phrase exactly, not a paraphrase.

## Self-Check

- Can I give a specific detail from a paragraph, not just a general idea, when asked "according to Paragraph
  ___"?
- Can I find and quote the exact phrase that answers a question, rather than describing it in my own words?
- Can I explain what "before long" means and use it correctly in a new sentence?
- Can I describe something by comparing it to something else and point to the specific reason the text gives?
